Controls

Move
WASD or arrows. On touch, drag from the lower left.
Paint
Drag on the paper, or hold Space while moving. Release to cast.
Dodge
Shift, right-click the arena, or tap Dodge. Aim with movement. Dodge through a creature to damage it and recover ink. The ring shows the cooldown.
Pause
Menu, Esc, or P.
Ward
Close a loop to slow and damage creatures inside.
Burst
Cross two fresh straight lines near their middles.
Chain
Defeat creatures in quick succession. Every third in a chain restores bonus ink.
The Blot
Use wards and bursts against its armor. Its red rings widen below half health.
