Controls & credits
  • WASD or arrows roll. I/J/K/L aim the moonlight. Q/E orbit. Escape pauses or resumes.
  • Point to aim, left-drag to roll, and right-drag to orbit.
  • On touch, drag on the left to roll and on the right to aim. Drag a second finger on the right to orbit.
  • The left stick or D-pad rolls, the right stick aims in any direction, and the bumpers orbit. Start pauses or resumes. Y/△ returns to the clearing. In the menu, use the D-pad to choose, A/× to select and B/○ to resume.
  • VR controllers: left stick rolls, right stick aims. Hold right grip while using the right stick to orbit. A right face button pauses or resumes. A left face button returns to the clearing.
  • Tracked hands: pinch thumb and index finger and drag, left to roll or right to aim. Right thumb–ring drag orbits. Pinch thumb and middle finger on the right to pause or resume, or on the left to return.
