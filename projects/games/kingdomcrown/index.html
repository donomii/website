FIELD NOTES FOR THE UNBURIED

How to haunt a kingdom

Ride and gather

Click or tap the scene to ride to that point. The steed gallops toward distant points and slows as it approaches or needs to recover. The gold bar shows stamina and turns purple while the steed recovers from exhaustion.

Spend Dread

Ride through glowing wisps to collect Dread. Click a nearby standing stone or its action button to awaken it for 8 Dread. The same action upgrades it for 14 and then 22 Dread.

Feed the Broods

Active Broods automatically hatch crawlers. Higher levels are tougher, hatch faster, and eventually call brutes. Their small bars appear only after taking damage.

Mark the outcasts

Select the action at a nearby roadside fire and spend 3 Dread to Mark its outcast. New Acolytes walk to open structure jobs. Empty fires attract another outcast after 22 seconds.

Raise staffed growths

Click a nearby foundation or its action button to build or upgrade. Pastures make Dread, Spires attack, Barricades block and repair, and Charnel Yards turn human remains into bonus Dread. A growth works only after its Acolyte arrives.

Break the crusade

Broods and staffed Spires fight for you. Use Barricades to slow the attackers. Archers fire at range and torchbearers do extra damage to structures.

Guard the Heart

Heart is base health. Zero ends the run. The violet bar under the rider is Veil. Recover it near an unthreatened Heart. At zero Veil, you spill Dread and reform at the Heart.

Complete the rite

Raid shows where the next attack will enter and when. Orange marks on the map track crusaders. With all four Broods awake, click the ritual at the Heart to spend 16 Dread, then hold off the final assault.

Click the bottom map to ride farther. It shows your position, the Heart, Broods, fires, and structures. A structure's job mark is red when vacant, gold while its worker travels, and teal when staffed. Use Menu to pause or change sound. Leaving the window also pauses and clears your destination.
