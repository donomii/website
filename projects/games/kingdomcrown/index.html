How to play

Ride and gather

Hold the left mouse button or your finger on either side of the rider. On a keyboard use Left and Right. On a gamepad use the left stick or D-pad. Release to stop. A longer hold starts a gallop.

Spend Dread

Ride through wisps to refill your purse. Tap a highlighted site, its button, Up on the keyboard, or A (Cross) on a gamepad to send one coin. Hold to send more. Ride away to get unfinished payments back.

Broods

Active Broods automatically hatch crawlers. Higher levels are tougher, hatch faster, and eventually call brutes. Their small bars appear only after taking damage.

Recruit workers

Recruit an Acolyte for 3 Dread at a roadside fire. Acolytes walk to open structure jobs. Empty fires attract another outcast after 22 seconds.

Build

Pay at any highlighted site to build or upgrade it. Staffed Pastures pay Dread directly, Spires attack, Barricades repair, and Charnel Yards harvest remains.

Defend

Broods and staffed Spires attack automatically. Barricades shield allies behind them, even from archers. Torchbearers do extra damage to structures.

Guard the Heart

Heart is base health. Zero ends the run. The violet bar under the rider is Veil. Recover it near an unthreatened Heart. At zero Veil, you spill Dread and reform at the Heart.

Final siege

Awaken four Broods and spend 16 Dread at the Heart to start the siege. Defend both sides and watch the Raid readout for reinforcements.

Use Enter or A (Cross) to start, resume, or replay. Use Escape or the gamepad Start (Options) button to pause or resume. The bottom map shows your position and landmarks. Job marks are red when vacant, gold while a worker travels, and teal when staffed.
