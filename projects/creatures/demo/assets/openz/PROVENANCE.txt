The 276 original FBX files and 47 original GLB files in originals/ are unchanged.
catalog.json records their paths relative to Assets/OpenZ and the SHA-256 of each original.
The viewer resolves their external images to reduced WebP copies of the source artwork.
Raw files show the model as exported. The Unity project assembles additional materials, prefabs and effects.

The nineteen GLBs in actors/ are adaptations under the same license. The BS-01 Grunt and six BS01V3 robot bodies carry their matching weapon on RightHandWeapon. The Grunt wears the original head, hip and back kit from BS01_Type_Grunt.fbx. Red and blue team colors are baked through the original alpha masks. The Grunt and shared robot movement use BS01_Robot_Anims.fbx. The specialized robot poses come from the respective body FBX files. Wildlife comes from Resources/OpenZ/Critters and Resources/OpenZ/L16Animals. The Mini Raptor uses the Raptor mesh with the original raptor_mini_tex.png skin. Web adaptations crop two loops, resize textures, adapt Unity materials, simplify and quantize mesh data, resample animation and normalize facing for Creatures.
