The Courtyard

Choose an exhibit to visit.

Controls

Drag to look · Hold right mouse button, WASD, or arrow keys to walk through doorways · Choose an exhibit to go directly there

Gamepad · Left stick walks, right stick looks. D-pad up/down highlights controls; left/right changes the exhibit choice. A/Cross visits it or selects the crosshair target.

VR · Left thumbstick to walk, right thumbstick to turn. Point at the floor and pull the trigger or pinch to teleport through open doorways. Point at exhibits and buttons to select them.

Asteroid Farmer · Select soil to plant or harvest and structures to build. Aim the crosshair and press Space or A/Cross, or click, trigger or pinch directly.

Station · Choose a module blueprint on the console, select a room, then a glowing port to extend it. An occupied neighboring cell connects a tube. Click, trigger, pinch, Space or A/Cross selects the target. Limit: 96 modules; each Habitat adds 3 crew, up to 24.
